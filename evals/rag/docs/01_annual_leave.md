# Annual Leave Policy

Example Parcels Ltd gives every employee paid time off to rest. This policy covers how much leave you get, how to book it and what happens to leave you do not use.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive the same entitlement in proportion to their contracted hours. After five years of continuous service, the entitlement rises to 27 days.

## Booking leave

Book leave in the HR portal at least two weeks before the first day you want off. Your line manager approves or declines the request within five working days. Requests for more than ten consecutive working days need approval from your department head as well.

## Peak season

Between 15 November and 5 January, warehouse and delivery staff may take at most three consecutive days of leave. Office staff are not affected by this restriction.

## Carrying leave over

You may carry up to five unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they are lost. Leave is never paid out instead of being taken, except when you leave the company.
