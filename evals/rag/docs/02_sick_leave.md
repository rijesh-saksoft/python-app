# Sick Leave Policy

This policy explains what to do when you are too ill to work, and how sick pay works at Example Parcels Ltd.

## Reporting an absence

Call your line manager before your shift starts, or within one hour of your usual start time if you work in the office. A text message is not enough on the first day; speak to someone. Tell them how long you expect to be away.

## Evidence

For absences of up to seven calendar days you complete a self-certification form in the HR portal when you return. From the eighth calendar day you must provide a doctor's note.

## Sick pay

Employees with more than six months of service receive full pay for the first 10 working days of sickness in any rolling 12-month period, and half pay for the next 10 working days. After that, only statutory sick pay applies.

## Return to work

After any absence longer than three working days, your line manager holds a short return-to-work meeting on your first day back. The meeting checks whether you need adjustments; it is not a disciplinary step.
