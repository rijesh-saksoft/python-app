# Overtime and Shift Policy

Depots run around the clock. This policy explains how shifts are planned and how extra hours are paid.

## Rotas

Shift rotas are published at least 14 days in advance in the workforce app. Changes after publication need the employee's agreement, except in an emergency.

## Overtime

Overtime must be approved by the shift supervisor before it is worked. Weekday overtime is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate. Overtime on Sundays and public holidays is paid at 2 times the normal rate. Office staff on salaries above the management threshold receive time off in lieu instead of overtime pay.

## Night shifts

Hours worked between 22:00 and 06:00 carry a night premium of 15 percent on top of the normal rate. Night workers are offered a free health assessment every year.

## Shift swaps

Employees may swap shifts with a colleague who has the same skills and licences. Record the swap in the workforce app at least 48 hours before the shift.

## Rest

Every shift longer than six hours includes an unpaid break of at least 30 minutes. There must be at least 11 hours of rest between the end of one shift and the start of the next.
