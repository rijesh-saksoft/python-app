# Code of Conduct

This code sets out the behaviour Example Parcels Ltd expects from everyone who works for or with the company.

## Respect

Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated, whether in person, by message or on social media.

## Conflicts of interest

Declare any interest that could influence your work decisions, such as a family member working for a supplier, in the HR portal within 30 days of it arising. Your manager decides whether you must step back from related decisions.

## Social media

You may say where you work, but never share customer information, photos taken inside depots or confidential company plans. Only the communications team speaks for the company in public.

## Disciplinary process

Breaches of this code are handled through the disciplinary process. A first minor breach normally leads to a verbal warning, a repeated breach to a written warning, and serious misconduct, such as theft, violence or deliberately hiding a security incident, may lead to dismissal.

## Raising concerns

If you see behaviour that breaks this code, tell your manager or HR. If you cannot raise it openly, use the Whistleblowing Policy.
