# Company Equipment Policy

Laptops, phones, scanners and other equipment belong to Example Parcels Ltd. This policy explains how to look after them.

## What you receive

Office staff receive a laptop, a monitor and a headset. Drivers receive a handheld scanner and a company phone. Laptops are replaced every four years and phones every three years, or sooner if they fail.

## Looking after equipment

Keep equipment with you or locked away. Never leave a laptop or scanner visible in a parked vehicle. Personal software may not be installed; ask the IT service desk for any tool you need.

## Loss, theft and damage

Report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk within 24 hours, and to the police for theft, so the device can be locked and wiped remotely. Accidental damage is repaired at no cost to you. If equipment is lost twice in 12 months through carelessness, your manager may refer the case to HR.

## Leaving the company

Return all equipment on your last working day. Equipment that is not returned within 10 working days is deducted from your final pay at its current value, as agreed in your contract.
