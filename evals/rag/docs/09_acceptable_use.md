# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy covers how you may use company email, internet access, messaging tools and AI tools.

## Personal use

Limited personal use of company internet and email is allowed during breaks, as long as it does not affect your work or break the law. Personal files must not be stored on company drives.

## Email and messaging

Use only company email and the company messaging tool for work. Do not forward work email to a personal account. Customer data may only be shared through approved company systems.

## AI tools

Only AI tools approved by IT may be used for work; the current list is on the intranet. Never paste customer names, addresses, tracking numbers or other personal data into any AI tool that is not on the approved list. You remain responsible for checking any text or code an AI tool produces before you use it.

## Monitoring

Company systems are monitored to detect security threats. Monitoring is automatic; individual messages are only read when a security incident or formal investigation requires it, and only with approval from HR and the Data Protection Officer.

## Breaches

Breaking this policy may lead to access being removed and to disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct.
