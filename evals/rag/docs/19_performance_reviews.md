# Performance Review Policy

Performance reviews help employees and managers agree on goals, give feedback and plan development.

## Review cycle

Every employee has a yearly review in April and a mid-year check-in in October. New employees have a probation review at the end of their third month instead of the first yearly review.

## Goals

Each employee agrees three to five goals with their manager at the start of the year. Goals are recorded in the HR portal and may be updated at the mid-year check-in.

## Ratings

Reviews use four ratings: exceeds expectations, meets expectations, partly meets expectations and does not meet expectations. A calibration meeting of managers in each department checks that ratings are applied fairly before they are shared.

## Pay

Pay rises take effect on 1 July and depend on the yearly rating and the company's results. Employees rated "does not meet expectations" do not receive a performance-based rise that year.

## Improvement plans

An employee rated "does not meet expectations" receives a performance improvement plan of up to 12 weeks, with clear goals and weekly check-ins. Training needs are agreed under the Training and Development Policy.

## Appeals

You may appeal your rating to HR within 10 working days of receiving it.
