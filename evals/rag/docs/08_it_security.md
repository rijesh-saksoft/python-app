# IT Security Policy

Attackers target logistics companies because one stolen account can redirect deliveries or leak customer addresses. These rules protect our systems.

## Passwords

Use a passphrase of at least 14 characters for your company account. Do not reuse a password from any other service. Passwords do not expire on a schedule; change yours at once if you think someone else knows it.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required for every company account, including email, the HR portal and the depot systems. Use the company authenticator app; text message codes are only allowed for drivers' handheld scanners.

## Software updates

Laptops install security updates automatically. Restart your laptop at least once a week so updates can finish. A device that is more than 14 days behind on updates is blocked from the network until it is updated.

## Phishing

Never enter your password after following a link in an email. Report suspicious emails with the Report button in the mail client. If you clicked a link or entered your password, report it straight away as a security incident; see the Incident Reporting Policy.

## Administrator rights

Administrator rights on laptops are granted only to IT staff. Developers may request a separate administrator account, valid for 90 days at a time.
