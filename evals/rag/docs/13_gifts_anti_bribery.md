# Gifts and Anti-Bribery Policy

Example Parcels Ltd does not offer, pay or accept bribes, directly or through anyone else. This policy applies to all employees, contractors and agents.

## Gifts and hospitality you receive

You may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to 50 from a supplier or customer. Anything worth more must be declined politely, or, if refusing would cause offence, accepted and handed to the compliance team. Cash or vouchers that work like cash are never accepted, whatever the amount.

## Gifts you give

Gifts to clients are limited to 50 per person per year and must be recorded in the gifts register. Never give a gift to a public official, including customs and port staff.

## Facilitation payments

Small unofficial payments to speed up routine actions, such as releasing a parcel from customs, are bribes and are forbidden, even where they are common practice. The only exception is a payment demanded under threat to someone's safety; report it to the compliance team within 24 hours.

## Gifts register

Record every gift or hospitality worth more than 20, given or received, in the gifts register within 10 working days. The compliance team reviews the register every quarter.

## Reporting

Report any request for a bribe to the compliance team, or anonymously through the Whistleblowing Policy.
