# Training and Development Policy

Example Parcels Ltd wants everyone to keep learning. This policy covers compulsory training and support for further study.

## Compulsory training

Every employee completes the induction course, data protection training and IT security training in their first month, and refreshes data protection and security training every year. Depot staff also complete manual handling training; drivers complete a defensive driving course within three months of starting.

## Development budget

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,000 per year for courses, books and conferences related to their role. Requests are approved by the line manager in the learning portal.

## Study leave

Employees on an approved qualification receive up to 5 days of paid study leave per year for exams and preparation.

## Repaying fees

If the company pays more than 2,000 for a qualification and you leave within 24 months of finishing it, you repay part of the cost: the full amount within 12 months, and half between 12 and 24 months. This does not apply if the company ends your employment.

## Records

All completed training is recorded in the learning portal. Managers review training records during the yearly performance review.
