# Whistleblowing Policy

Anyone working for Example Parcels Ltd can report serious wrongdoing safely, including anonymously, without fear of retaliation.

## What to report

Report fraud, bribery, danger to health and safety, environmental damage, breaking the law, or an attempt to cover any of these up. Personal complaints about your own employment, such as pay or a disagreement with your manager, belong to the grievance procedure instead.

## How to report

Use the independent speak-up line, which is run by an outside provider and open 24 hours a day by phone or web form. You may give your name or stay anonymous. You can also report directly to the Head of Compliance.

## What happens next

The Head of Compliance acknowledges a report within 7 days and gives feedback on the outcome within 3 months, as far as confidentiality allows. Anonymous reporters receive a reference number to follow up through the speak-up line.

## Protection

Nobody may be dismissed, demoted or treated worse because they made a report in good faith, even if it turns out to be mistaken. Retaliating against a whistleblower is gross misconduct. Knowingly making a false report is also a disciplinary matter.
