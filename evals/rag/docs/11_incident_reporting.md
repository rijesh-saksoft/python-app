# Incident Reporting Policy

An incident is any event that harms, or could harm, people, customer data, company systems or parcels in our care. Reporting quickly limits the damage.

## What to report

Report security incidents such as a clicked phishing link, a lost device or suspicious account activity. Report data incidents such as personal data sent to the wrong person. Report safety incidents such as injuries and near misses under the Health and Safety Policy.

## How to report

Security and data incidents: call the IT service desk on the 24-hour number, or use the Report Incident form on the intranet. Do this within one hour of noticing the incident, day or night. Do not try to investigate or fix it yourself, and do not delete any evidence.

## What happens next

The security team confirms receipt within 30 minutes and assigns a severity from 1 (critical) to 4 (low). Severity 1 and 2 incidents are escalated to the Chief Information Security Officer immediately. If personal data is involved, the Data Protection Officer decides whether the regulator must be told.

## No blame

People who report their own mistakes promptly are not disciplined for the mistake itself. Hiding an incident, however, is a serious breach of the Code of Conduct.
