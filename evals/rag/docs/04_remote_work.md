# Remote Work Policy

Office roles at Example Parcels Ltd can be done partly from home. Warehouse, depot and delivery roles cannot, because the work is on site.

## Eligibility

Office employees who have passed probation may work from home up to three days per week. New employees work in the office full time during their first three months so they can learn the team and the systems.

## Core hours

Everyone, at home or in the office, is available between 10:00 and 15:00 on working days. Outside core hours you may arrange your time with your manager.

## Working abroad

You may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Request it in the HR portal at least four weeks in advance, because tax and data protection checks are needed. Some countries are not allowed at all.

## Home set-up

The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset; see the Company Equipment Policy. A home-office allowance of 300 is paid once, after probation, towards a desk or chair. Keep the receipts; the allowance is claimed through the Expenses Policy process.

## Security at home

Never work on company data on a shared or public computer, and lock your screen whenever you leave it. Public Wi-Fi is only allowed with the company VPN switched on.
