# Data Protection Policy

Example Parcels Ltd handles personal data for millions of deliveries: names, addresses, phone numbers and sometimes signatures and photos. This policy explains how we protect it.

## Principles

Collect only the personal data a task needs, use it only for that task, and keep it only as long as necessary. Access to customer data is limited to staff whose role requires it.

## Retention

Delivery records with customer addresses are kept for 13 months after delivery and then deleted automatically. Proof-of-delivery photos are kept for 90 days. Employee records are kept for six years after the employee leaves.

## Customer requests

Customers may ask to see, correct or delete their personal data. Forward every such request to the Data Protection Officer on the day you receive it. The company must answer within one month.

## Sharing data

Personal data may be shared with another company only under a signed data processing agreement checked by the Data Protection Officer. Never send customer lists by email attachment.

## Data breaches

A lost device, a misdirected email containing personal data or any unauthorised access is a possible data breach. Report it immediately using the Incident Reporting Policy. The company may have to notify the regulator within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, so do not wait until you are sure.
