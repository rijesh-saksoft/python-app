# Expenses Policy

Example Parcels Ltd repays reasonable costs you pay yourself while doing your job. This policy explains what can be claimed and how.

## Making a claim

Submit claims in the finance portal within 30 days of paying. Attach a photo of every receipt. Claims older than 90 days are not paid. Finance pays approved claims with the next monthly salary.

## Meals

Meals are claimable only when you work away from your usual site for more than five hours without an overnight stay. The limit is 25 per day. For trips with an overnight stay, the daily meal limit in the Business Travel Policy applies instead.

## Mileage

Business journeys in your own car are paid at 0.45 per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year and 0.25 per mile after that. Your usual journey to work is never claimable.

## Client entertainment

Entertaining clients needs your manager's approval in advance. The limit is 75 per guest, and every guest must be named on the claim. Gifts to clients follow the Gifts and Anti-Bribery Policy.

## What is never paid

Fines, personal phone bills, alcohol outside approved client entertainment, and tips above 10 percent are never reimbursed.
