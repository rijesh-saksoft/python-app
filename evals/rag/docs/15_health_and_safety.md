# Health and Safety Policy

Depots and vehicles are busy places with heavy loads and moving machines. Everyone has a duty to keep themselves and others safe.

## Personal protective equipment

In every depot, high-visibility vests and safety boots are compulsory. Gloves are required when handling cages and damaged parcels. The company provides protective equipment free of charge and replaces it when worn.

## Manual handling

Do not lift any parcel heavier than 23 kg alone. Parcels over 23 kg carry a red heavy label and must be moved by two people or with lifting equipment. Manual handling training is completed on the first day and refreshed every two years.

## Forklifts

Only staff holding a current company forklift licence may drive a forklift. Pedestrians keep to the marked walkways at all times.

## Accidents and near misses

Report every injury, however small, and every near miss to your shift supervisor before the end of your shift, and record it in the safety app. Serious injuries are also reported to the regulator by the health and safety team.

## Fire

Fire alarms are tested every Monday at 10:00. When the alarm sounds at any other time, leave by the nearest exit and go to the assembly point; do not collect belongings.
