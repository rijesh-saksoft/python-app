# Business Travel Policy

This policy covers booking and paying for business trips. Claims for costs during a trip follow the Expenses Policy.

## Approval

Every trip needs your manager's approval before anything is booked. Trips abroad also need approval from the department head and must be booked at least 21 days in advance unless there is an urgent business reason.

## Booking

Book all flights, trains and hotels through the company travel portal. Bookings made elsewhere are not reimbursed, even if they were cheaper, because the company must know where its travellers are in an emergency.

## Class of travel

Trains and flights are booked in standard or economy class. Flights longer than six hours may be booked in premium economy. Business class is never booked for internal trips.

## Hotels

The hotel limit is 150 per night in most cities and 220 per night in capital cities. Breakfast may be included in the room rate.

## Meals while travelling

On a trip with an overnight stay, you may spend up to 60 per day on meals, including any breakfast not included with the room. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Safety

Register every trip abroad in the travel portal and keep your phone switched on. In an emergency call the 24-hour travel assistance number printed on the back of your company ID card.
