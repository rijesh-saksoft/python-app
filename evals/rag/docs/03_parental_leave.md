# Parental Leave Policy

Example Parcels Ltd supports employees who become parents through birth, adoption or surrogacy.

## Primary carer leave

The primary carer may take up to 52 weeks of leave. The company pays full salary for the first 16 weeks and half salary for the following 10 weeks. The rest of the leave is unpaid.

## Secondary carer leave

The secondary carer receives 4 weeks of leave at full pay. It must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement, in blocks of at least one week.

## Notice

Tell HR at least 15 weeks before the expected week of birth, or within one week of being matched for adoption. Confirm the dates you plan to take in writing.

## Keeping in touch

During leave you may work up to 10 keeping-in-touch days, paid at your normal rate. These days are optional and must be agreed with your manager. They do not end or extend your leave.

## Returning

You return to the same role if your leave was 26 weeks or less. After a longer leave you return to the same role or, if that is not possible, a similar role on the same terms. You may request flexible working on your return; see the Remote Work Policy.
