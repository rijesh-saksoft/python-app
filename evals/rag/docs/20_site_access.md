# Visitor and Site Access Policy

Depots hold parcels of high value, and offices hold customer data. Access to all sites is controlled.

## ID badges

Wear your company ID badge visibly at all times on site. Do not hold doors open for people you do not recognise. A lost badge must be reported to security on the same day so it can be cancelled.

## Visitors

Register visitors in the reception system at least one working day in advance. Visitors sign in, receive a visitor badge and are accompanied by their host at all times. Visitors may not enter sorting areas without safety boots and a high-visibility vest, which reception lends out.

## Contractors

Contractors working on site for more than five days receive a temporary badge valid for at most 30 days. They complete the site safety induction before starting work.

## Restricted areas

The high-value cage, the server room and the security office are restricted areas. Entry is limited to named staff, and every entry is logged. Photography is forbidden in all restricted areas.

## Out of hours

Access between 22:00 and 06:00 is only possible for staff on a scheduled shift. Anyone else must be authorised in advance by the site manager.
