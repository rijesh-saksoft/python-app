# Vehicle and Driver Policy

This policy applies to everyone who drives a company van or truck, and to employees who drive their own car on company business.

## Licence checks

Drivers' licences are checked before employment starts and every six months after that. Tell your manager within 24 hours if you receive penalty points or a driving ban.

## Driving hours

Drivers may drive for at most 9 hours a day, with a 45-minute break after every 4.5 hours of driving. Daily rest must be at least 11 hours between shifts.

## Phones and navigation

Hand-held phone use while driving is forbidden, including at traffic lights. The company phone runs navigation in a fixed cradle. Delivery scans are made only when the vehicle is parked with the engine off.

## Vehicle checks

Complete the walk-around check in the driver app before the first journey of each day: tyres, lights, mirrors, load security and fluids. A vehicle with a failed safety item must not be driven; report it to the fleet team.

## Accidents

After a road accident, stop, make the scene safe and call emergency services if anyone is hurt. Then call the fleet team and complete the accident form in the driver app the same day. Never admit liability at the scene.

## Fines

Parking and speeding fines received while driving a company vehicle are the driver's responsibility and are not reimbursed.
